Quick Rules

Official turn rule

Optional peace first. Then choose exactly one core action: Conquer, Expand, Sail, Technology, or Influence. Spend territorial action points by tapping legal adjacent regions. End Turn discards unused points.

Scoring

Land region: 2 VP each. Sea zone: 1 VP each. Technology: Technology ÷ 2 VP. Influence: Influence ÷ 4 VP. Capturing a capital: +10 VP. Recurring perks and faction abilities may add more.

Capital protection

Active capitals cannot be conquered during Rounds 1–4. Protection ends at the start of Round 5.

The board

The illustrated map is the board. Land regions are strategic areas drawn from its own borders; broad sea zones cover the oceans. Adjacency follows a fixed graph (including sea lanes and the Pacific wrap-around), and a newly captured region can immediately open the next adjacent target, preserving Poleis-style chaining. A ! marks land cut off from its capital.

Resistance

A power with no land enters Resistance Mode. On its turn it rolls d4; 4+ restores its capital, awards +10 VP, and grants two turns of special immunity.
