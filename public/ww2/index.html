Quick Rules

Official turn rule

Optional peace first. Then choose exactly one core action: Conquer, Expand, Sail, Technology, or Influence. Spend territorial action points by clicking legal adjacent hexes. End Turn discards unused points.

Scoring

Land: 2 VP each. Sea: 1 VP each. Technology: Technology ÷ 2 VP. Influence: Influence ÷ 4 VP. Capturing a capital: +10 VP. Recurring perks and faction abilities may add more.

Capital protection

Active capitals cannot be conquered during Rounds 1–4. Protection ends at the start of Round 5.

Map scale

Each land hex is a strategic region, not a literal province. Sea hexes represent operational sea zones. Newly captured hexes can immediately open the next adjacent target, preserving Poleis-style chaining.

Resistance

A power with no land enters Resistance Mode. On its turn it rolls d4; 4+ restores its capital, awards +10 VP, and grants two turns of special immunity.
